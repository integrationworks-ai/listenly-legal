Delete your Listenly account

Listenly, by Integrationworks LLC — Last updated: November 14, 2026

Leer en español

You can delete your Listenly account and its data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

In the app

  • Open Listenly and sign in.
  • Tap your avatar (top right), then Privacy, then Delete my account.
  • Confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email listenly@integrationworksco.com from the email address on your Listenly account, with the subject "Delete my Listenly account". We reply to that address to confirm the request is yours, then delete the account within 30 days. If you signed in with Apple or Google and do not know which address the account uses, tell us and we will help you find it.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your profile (display name, timezone, language)
  • Your check-ins and notes, and the reactions on them
  • Reactions and notes you sent to your partner
  • The suggestions shown to you
  • Your notification settings, your device's notification token, and your AI consent record
  • Your link to your partner and your invite codes

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your partner's own account and their own check-ins are not deleted.

What is kept

  • Backups. Deleted data can remain in encrypted database backups for up to 30 days, after which the backups are replaced.
  • Purchases. Apple, Google and our payment processor (RevenueCat) keep their own records of purchases as the law requires. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

Delete some data, keep the account

In the app, under Privacy, you can delete your check-in history without deleting your account. You can ask for the same by email at the address above.
