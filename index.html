Listenly is a couples emotional check-in app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights.

Data we collect

Data you provide:

  • Account and profile — email address, password (stored hashed), display name, timezone and language.
  • Check-ins and reactions — the moods you select, optional notes, and the emoji or text you send your partner.
  • Settings and consent — notification preferences, quiet hours, and your AI consent choice.
  • Purchases — whether you have Listenly Plus or a theme. Apple or Google handles payment; we never see your card.
  • Optional answers — such as how you heard about Listenly.

Data recorded automatically:

  • Activity data — when you reach key early steps in the app, such as your first check-in or linking with your partner.
  • Diagnostic and usage records — short error codes when something in the app fails, which we delete after 30 days, and a count of AI suggestion requests, which we keep with your account to apply daily limits.
  • Push token — so we can send notifications. Removed when you sign out or delete your account.

How we use your data

  • To run the app: deliver check-ins and reactions to your linked partner, send notifications, and authenticate your account.
  • To provide AI suggestions and weekly reflections, if you opt in (see below).
  • To process purchases and subscriptions.
  • To understand how people use Listenly, using totals for our own planning, and to find and fix problems.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

AI features

AI suggestions and weekly reflections (Listenly Plus) are optional and work only if you opt in. Suggestions require both partners to opt in. You can turn consent off at any time under Privacy.

When they are on, we send Anthropic, PBC the mood and optional note of the check-in being responded to, or, for a weekly reflection, that week's check-ins. We do not send your email address, account ID or name. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, data sent this way is not used to train its models. The AI-written wording of well-performing suggestions, never your notes or any identifying details, may be added to a shared library shown to other users.

Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run Listenly, and only for that purpose. Each provider is required to protect your data in a way consistent with this policy.

  • Supabase — stores your data in the United States. Privacy policy.
  • Anthropic — AI features, as described above. Privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — manages purchases and subscriptions. Privacy policy.
  • Apple — push notifications and Sign in with Apple. Privacy policy.
  • Google and Firebase — push notifications, Google sign-in and app update settings. Privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — checks that sign-up and sign-in requests come from a person. See Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

These providers may also receive the technical device information any online service sees, such as IP address and device type.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. Check-in history is kept until you delete it or your account. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data in the app at your avatar (top right) → Settings → Privacy → Delete my account. Deletion cannot be undone. You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing the address below.

Your rights

Under Privacy in the app you can pause seeing your partner's check-ins, unlink from your partner, delete your check-in history, revoke AI consent, and delete your account. To exercise any other right under applicable privacy law, contact us below.

Children

Listenly is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and notify users in the app before the changes take effect.
